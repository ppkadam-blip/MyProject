Before you go

Travel lightly

A few local-minded notes to keep the week easy, respectful and sunny.

🚗

Plan for traffic

Short distances can take time. Use a private driver for temple days and leave a little margin.

🌊

Match the waves

Kuta and Seminyak suit beginners; Uluwatu, Bingin and Padang-Padang suit stronger surfers.

🙏

Dress respectfully

Bring a sarong or borrow one at temples, and cover shoulders and knees before entering.

✈️

Sleep near departure

For an early flight, swap Ubud for Jimbaran, Sanur or an airport-area hotel on the last night.
